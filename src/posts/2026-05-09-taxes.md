Would you pay your grocery store a fee to buy groceries?

"We," they might say, "take the complexity out of finding farmers and sourcing products. We make it easy to buy groceries by putting fresh produce and tasty snacks."

In the US, we pay private companies to help us pay our taxes. It doesn't need to be that way.

People complain about taxes, both paying them and, in at least the two countries that I'm required to, prepare them.

I generally support the idea of taxes, and of paying them! Taxes allow us to meet the needs of the society we are a part of, at a cost proportional to what we can pay.

What we can critique, is the complexity of paying taxes. Complexity means that:

1. An industry that [profits on this complexity exists.](https://itep.org/intuit-helped-limit-americans-tax-filing-options-while-raking-in-millions-in-tax-breaks/)
2. The richest amongst us (definitely not you, the reader of this blog) find gaps to avoid paying taxes, sometimes entirely.
3. You spend hours or days struggling to give the government (an extension of our society) money.
