I lived in China from 2016-2019, and visited in late 2025. Some thoughts.

*   Basically cashless and more digital
    
*   Much quieter (EVs)
    
*   Same prices as nearly a decade ago, unlike the US
    
*   Smoking is still everywhere, including inside
    
*   Easier to travel without speaking Chinese, equally rewarding if you do
