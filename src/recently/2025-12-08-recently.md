My wife and I recently finished a 3+ month trip to Asia. We were incredibly fortunate to have employers that let us take (unpaid) time off work for so long.

This post could be novel, but in summary, we saw so many things, ate so much good food, and generally had a rejuvenating time away from work and our daily routines.

We spent about half of the trip in China, visiting megacities, remote villages and everything in between, zipping around primarily by train. While aiming to include down time, much of this part of the trip was focused on experiences — visiting rice terraces, climbing the great wall, etc. Our time in China concluded in Tibet, where we took an overland tour from Lhasa to Kathmandu. We then spent almost two weeks hiking around the majestic Manaslu peak on the Manaslu Circuit, bringing us from massive gorges up over a 5,100m pass. We spent the rest of the time off relaxing in Thailand, visiting three islands in the south and fully enjoying the tropical weather.

Some takeaways include:

1.  Taking extended time off is just wonderful. I encourage anyone who's able to, to do so.
    
2.  Travel, and leaving daily routines of home, helps put many things into perspective.
    
3.  Three months, for us, is an optimal amount of time away. Both of us are ready (in a positive way) to return to our daily routines and are excited about work and seeing friend, but was enough to fully get away from our normal lives.
