My first recently post! I hope to write roughly monthly about what I've been up to, what I hope to be doing, and somethings other things on my mind.

As of December, we've been in Luzern, Switzerland for one year. It's a beautiful town and has wonderful access to nature, which we've been trying to take advantage of as much as possible. It's been extremely foggy for almost the past two months, making it all-the-more tempting to get above the clouds.

{% image "./src/assets/img/recently/above_clouds.jpg", "above-clouds", "(min-width: 30em) 50 vw, 100vw" %}

It has been difficult to meet people, but I have a good network of friends around the country to rely on. This has been nice for planned visits, but spontaneous meet-ups or encounters have definitely been lacking.

I'm taking German classes twice per week with [Lingoda](https://learn.lingoda.com/en/referral/prxumk), an online language course platform. Progress is slow, but I've recently been able to understand simple questions and can sometimes slowly reply. Twice per week should have me progressing at one [CEFR level per year](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/level-descriptions).

Coding has been very fun and enjoyable recently. Hacking away in a Jupyter Notebook or building a simple web map has become easier, which has allowed me to (slowly) turn the infinite list of project ideas I have into reality. For example, [here's a map](https://wrynearson.github.io/watermap/) of all water fountains and toilets (on OSM) in Europe [GitHub repo](https://github.com/wrynearson/watermap).
