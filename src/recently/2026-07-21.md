Since my last post, the rest of Winter, Spring and Summer went by so quickly, almost concerningly-so.

After our annual company meeting in D.C. in late February, we said goodbye to the company office. Our company is fully distributed, which still 4 years on people ask me if I'm lonely or unproductive. Overall, working remotely and distributed-ly has been a fantastic experience due to the autonomy of my time, emphasis on outcomes and writing, and colleagues I've formed connections with around the world. This does, of course, come at the cost of those spontaneous encounters or connection of ideas you make with unstructured social time. For that, and to get out of the apartment more, I've started going to a co-working space in Zürich and meeting my one other Swiss-based colleague in three dimensions.

Socializing in our (Swiss) German-speaking region remains difficult, but I'm slowly (mostly through my partner) building up a local network. I've completed my A2 course in June and will continue with a B1 course this Autumn. While I get frustrated with the slow pace of my improvements, it is nice to see that I am making some progress — a reminder to balance motivation with patience.
