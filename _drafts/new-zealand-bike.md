## Sunday, 10 November, 2013

The trip begins! After a frantic Saturday & Sunday morning, said our goodbyes. Threw everything in the Van & left Christchurch. The drive to Picton was beautiful, as always.

We had great weather for the boat ride. Like Bainbridge & Seattle ferry, but 3 hours! Late at night, made it to Wellington. Also, on the boat deck, was really thinking of how beautiful & lucky I was to be in NZ, and all the great experiences.

## Monday, 11 November, 2013

Explored Wellington! Wandered around. Went to the 'Te Papa' museum. Saw the Gov't buildings, etc. Cool city! Left the afternoon for the Tongariro crossing.

## Tuesday, 12 November, 2013

Woke the to Mt. Doom shrouded in clouds, peak visible. After while started the Tongariro crossinging w/ Heiko, Andi & Sarah an the other. The place looks extraterrestriel! Lots of geothermal activity. Smelled of sulfur! Very Lool open pits, red earth, & Mt. Doom! Went to take Taupo Lots of Swans!
