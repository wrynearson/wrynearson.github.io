In October, my wife and I hiked along much of the Fisherman's Trail in Southwest Portugal. The full length of the trail is 226 km, winding between the Atlantic and the vibrant and desolate areas of the end of Europe. We ended up hiking nearly 100km between beautiful coastal towns and endless seaside cliffs.
