In US President Joe Biden's [speech](https://www.nytimes.com/2022/02/22/world/europe/biden-ukraine-speech-transcript.html) in reaction to Russia's invasion of Ukraine, fossil fuel supply and price disruption was mentioned:

> We’re monitoring energy supplies for any disruption. We’re executing a plan in coordination with major oil-producing consumers and producers toward a collective investment to secure stability and global energy supplies. This will be — this will blunt gas prices. I want to limit the pain to the American people are feeling at the gas pump. This is critical to me.

Despite drivers only paying about half of what drivers in western Europe do [^1]...

This raises a question that has been repeated for at least the past 50 years: *Why can't countries secure their energy supply?*

- Petrol price in US vs Europe.
