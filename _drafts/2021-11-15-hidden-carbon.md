# Hidden Sustainability

## Overview

- Peoples' sustainability focus is often on the most visible, but low impact solution (e.g., plastic straws).
- Present big driver's of the climate crisis both from individuals' actions, but more importantly, systemic drivers.
- Show the solution (not just reduction and cutting back)
- Turn this into a series of posts?

## Topics

- Energy [OurWorldInData](https://ourworldindata.org/emissions-by-sector#sector-by-sector-where-do-global-greenhouse-gas-emissions-come-from)
  - 72% of global emissions
  - Transportation
    - Only 5% of US communiters use public transportation (the rest drive) [theB1M](https://youtu.be/uyQjnW5ZQp8?t=341)
    - Could increased start/stop traffic due to high number of stop signs and traffic lights in North America vs Europe (roundabouts) have a sizeable impact?
  - Fugitive emissions (5.8% global emissions)
    - Fugitive emissions from oil and gas extraction and transporation (flaring, leakage, etc.) are 3.9% global CO2, more than double that of the global aviation industry (1.9%)
    - Fugitive emissions from coal (similar to above) are 1.9%
    - One difference is that we can choose to fly, whereas "choosing" to not leak fuel/coal gases seems more distant
  - Energy use in agriculture machinery and fishing vessels is 1.7%, almost that of aviation.
- Waste (3.2%)
  - Organiic decomposition in wastewater (1.3%) and landfills (1.9)
