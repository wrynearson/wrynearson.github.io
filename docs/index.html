Recently

December, 2024

My first recently post! I hope to write roughly monthly about what I've been up to, what I hope to be doing, and somethings other things on my mind.

As of December, we've been in Luzern, Switzerland for one year. It's a beautiful town and has wonderful access to nature, which we've been trying to take advantage of as much as possible. It's been extremely foggy for almost the past two months, making it all-the-more tempting to get above the clouds.

It has been difficult to meet people, but I have a good network of friends around the country to rely on. This has been nice for planned visits, but spontaneous meet-ups or encounters have definitely been lacking.

To help with this, I'm taking German classes twice per week with Lingoda, an online language course platform. Progress is slow, but I've recently been able to understand simple questions and can sometimes slowly reply. Twice per week should have me progressing at one CEFR level per year.

Coding has been very fun and enjoyable recently. Hacking away in a Jupyter Notebook or building a simple web map has become easier, which has allowed me to (slowly) turn the infinite list of project ideas I have into reality. For example, here's a map of all water fountains and toilets (on OSM) in Europe GitHub repo. I do still rely on ChatGPT to do these, especially to start projects.

I was lucky enough to go to India recently for a work trip. We stuck around for an additional week to take advantage of the opportunity. It was an eye-opening experience. I was particularly impacted by the stark, visible divide between the rich and poor. Unimaginable wealth would be right next to unimaginable poverty. Life was on full display, all manners (good and bad).

I'm excited for the holidays, snow(sports), projects at work, and coding opportunities, and am content with my German progress.
